The next era of artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence is moving from an emerging technology into a powerful part of everyday business. Companies are increasingly using AI to improve productivity, analyze information and create entirely new products.

This shift is creating significant momentum across the technology industry. What once seemed experimental is becoming a core part of how modern companies operate.

The broader landscape is also changing quickly. Businesses must determine where AI can create genuine value rather than simply following trends.

For marketers, the opportunity is particularly remarkable . AI can help teams understand customers, generate ideas and systematically test new approaches.

However, sustainable progress still requires rigorous thinking. Technology alone does not create a successful product. Companies must understand users, markets and the problems they are trying to solve.
